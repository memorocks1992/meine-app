Privacy & Disclaimer

Privacy information

Queue Berlin does not use cookies. The app stores a randomly generated visitor ID in your browser's local storage to support reports, forum posts and likes. This ID is pseudonymous, not fully anonymous, and is sent with relevant submissions to our database provider, Supabase. Forum display names are chosen by users; please do not include personal or sensitive information.

Club reports and forum content are transmitted to Supabase to provide this service. Forum posts and replies are automatically deleted after 24 hours (the scheduled cleanup may take a few extra minutes). Other data may be retained as needed to operate the service. Hosting and database providers may process technical data, such as connection information, to deliver and secure the service.

You can remove the locally stored visitor ID by clearing this site's browser storage. Doing so may affect your ability to manage likes associated with the previous ID.

Content and liability

Posts and reports reflect the views of their authors and are not verified or endorsed by Queue Berlin. Users are responsible for the content they submit. We may remove content that violates our guidelines. Information about clubs, events, queues and prices is provided for general guidance only; it may be incomplete, inaccurate or out of date. Please verify important details directly with the relevant venue.

Queue Berlin is provided without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability or that user-submitted information is accurate. To the extent permitted by applicable law, the operator is not liable for user-generated content or decisions made in reliance on information on this site. This does not limit liability where it cannot legally be excluded.
